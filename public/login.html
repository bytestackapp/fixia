FAQ List

What is Fixia?
Fixia is a service marketplace that connects customers with trusted local service providers. You can browse, compare, and hire professionals for services like plumbing, electrical work, beauty, and IT support—all in one place.
How do I sign up as a provider?
Tap "Offer Services" on the sign-up page and complete the 5-step registration. A valid government-issued ID is required for verification.
Is Fixia free to use?
Yes! Fixia offers a free plan for customers. Service providers can choose from flexible packages based on their needs.
How do I reset my password?
On the login page, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your registered email. You'll receive a reset link shortly.
How do I contact a service provider?
Once logged in, browse providers in your area and tap their profile to view contact options and send a booking request.

  Privacy Policy

All user data is securely stored and will not be shared with third parties without explicit consent. Provider-submitted documents (such as government-issued IDs and permits) are collected and used solely for identity verification and platform security.

ByteStack Labs complies with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines, ensuring that all personal information is processed, protected, and handled in accordance with applicable data privacy regulations.
